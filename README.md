# 📊 Sales Performance & Insights Dashboard

## 🚀 Project Overview
This end-to-end data analysis project focuses on analyzing sales performance, profit margins, and customer behavior. The interactive Power BI dashboard transforms raw sales data into actionable business insights, helping stakeholders identify high-performing categories, understand regional trends, and optimize payment methods.

## 🎨 UI/UX Enhancements
* **High Contrast Design:** Applied a professional dark sidebar palette to make slicers and filters highly readable.
* **Optimized Slicers:** Re-formatted the dropdown and month selections using clean white text and clear indicators for maximum scannability.
* **Visual Hierarchy:** Arranged Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) with distinctive icons at the top for an immediate high-level summary.

## 🛠️ Tools & Tech Stack
* **Data Visualization & Modeling:** Microsoft Power BI
* **Calculations:** DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for calculating key metrics like Profit Margin and Weekend Sales%.
* **Data Source:** `Sales_Alteryx.xlsx` (Cleaned & prepared sales dataset).

## 📈 Key Business Insights
* **Healthy Profitability:** The business achieved a total revenue of **$476K** with an impressive profit margin of **75.29%**.
* **Top Revenue Generator:** **Electronics** is the leading product category in both profit and total revenue, followed closely by Clothing.
* **Geographical Dominance:** **California** and **Florida** represent the highest-grossing states, highlighting key geographical areas for targeted marketing campaigns.
* **Customer Behavior:** **Credit Cards** are the most preferred payment method among customers, indicating a strong trend toward digital transactions.
